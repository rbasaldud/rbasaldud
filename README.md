# Hi, I'm Rodrigo Basaldud 

Actuarial Science graduate from UNAM interested in statistical methodology, regression modelling, and applied statistics. Seeking an integrated PhD in statistics.

My research interests include generalized linear models, time series, survival analysis, statistical inference, multivariate analysis, and change-point detection methods, particularly in high-dimensional and time-dependent data.

I am also interested in statistical computing, reproducible research, and the computational implementation of statistical methodologies.

Currently, I am working on the detection of slow slip events (SSEs) in seismic data using change-point detection methods for multivariate time series.


## Research interests

- Statistical inference and regression modelling
- Generalized linear models and survival analysis
- Multivariate analysis
- Change-point detection and time series
- Statistical computing and R
